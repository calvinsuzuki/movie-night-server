Watch movies with friends, using Tailscale

Movie night streams your screen straight to your friends' browsers. Tailscale is the free app that connects your devices privately, so friends can reach your stream from anywhere without you opening ports on your router. It takes about five minutes the first time.

🍿 Your PC (Movie night) → 🔒 Tailscale private network → 📱💻 Friends' browsers

1 Set up Tailscale on your PC

You do this once, on the computer that shares the movie.

  1. Install Tailscale from tailscale.com/download. The Movie night installer offers to do this for you on Windows. On Ubuntu, run: 
    curl -fsSL https://tailscale.com/install.sh | sh
sudo tailscale up
  2. Sign in with an account you already have (Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Apple…). On Windows, click the Tailscale icon in the taskbar tray and choose Log in. Ubuntu prints a link to open instead.
  3. Open Movie night's popcorn menu. The status turns from Waiting for Tailscale to Sharing, and a link like http://100.x.y.z:8080 appears. That's your viewer link.

2 Let your friends in

Pick one of these. Both are done on the Tailscale website, in the admin console.

Share just your PC Recommended

  1. Open Machines.
  2. Click the ⋯ menu next to your computer → Share…
  3. Create an invite link and send it to a friend (one link per friend).

Friends keep their own Tailscale account and can only see this one computer.

Invite them to your network

  1. Open Users.
  2. Click Invite users and enter their e-mail.

Simpler for close friends or family, but they join your whole network, and free plans limit how many users you can have.

3 What each friend does

Send them this list together with the invite link.

  1. Install Tailscale: iPhone/iPad from the App Store, Android from Google Play, Windows/Mac/Linux from tailscale.com/download.
  2. Open the invite link you sent, sign in with any account, and accept it.
  3. Make sure Tailscale is connected: the switch is on in the app (on phones it shows up as a VPN).
  4. Open your viewer link (http://100.x.y.z:8080) in a browser and tap Join stream. Each friend sets their own volume with the slider.
💡 Movie night can't share Discord or TeamSpeak sound, so talk there freely while you watch. Friends won't hear themselves echoed back.

4 Movie time

  • Popcorn menu → Copy viewer link and paste it in your group chat.
  • Screen picks the monitor, Quality picks 480p / 720p / 1080p. If friends see stuttering, lower the quality or bitrate.
  • Sound lets you share all apps or only the ones you tick (for example just your browser or VLC).
  • Your internet upload is shared between viewers: about 5 Mbps per friend at 1080p.

? If something doesn't work

  • The link doesn't load: Tailscale must be connected on both your PC and the friend's device, and Movie night must say Sharing.
  • Laggy or blurry: on your PC run tailscale ping <friend's device>. If it says via DERP, the connection is going through a slower relay. Restarting Tailscale on both sides or trying another network often fixes it. Lowering Quality also helps.
  • No sound for a friend: they should tap the page once, check the volume slider, and on iPhone turn off silent mode.
  • Windows asked about the firewall: allow Movie night (the installer normally does this).
  • Ubuntu shows a black screen: log out and choose Ubuntu on Xorg with the ⚙ button on the login screen.
You can open this guide again any time from the popcorn menu → Tailscale guide.
