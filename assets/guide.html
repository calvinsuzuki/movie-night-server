Watch movies with your friends, right in their browser

Movie night streams your screen and sound to friends wherever they are. You send a link, they open it in any browser (phone, tablet or computer), and you decide who gets in. Friends don't install anything.

🍿 You click Start sharing → 🔗 Send the link → 🙋 Friend asks to join → ✓ You allow → 📺 They watch

1 Start sharing

On the computer that plays the movie.

  1. Click the popcorn icon in the taskbar tray (on Windows it may hide behind the ^ arrow).
  2. Pick Screen (the monitor where the movie plays) and, if you like, Quality.
  3. Click Start sharing. After a few seconds your link is ready.
  4. Click Copy invite link and paste it in your group chat.

2 Let friends in

When a friend opens the link, they type their name and ask to join.

  • You hear a chime and a popup asks “Alice” wants to watch the movie. Let them in? Click Yes or No.
  • Missed it? The popcorn icon shows a blue dot, and the menu has ✓ Let “Alice” in / ✗ Deny. Requests you don't answer expire after 2 minutes.
  • Friends hear a short chime when someone else joins. The corner of their page shows how many are watching; hovering it (or tapping, on a phone) lists everyone's names.
  • Allowed friends see the movie within a couple of seconds. Each one sets their own volume.
  • Watching lists everyone connected. Click a name to remove them.
💡 The popup appears on your main screen, so if that's the screen you share, friends see it for a moment too. Prefer no knocking at all? Turn off Network → Ask me before anyone joins, and anyone with the link gets straight in.

3 When the movie's over

  • Pause picture freezes the picture and sound; friends stay connected.
  • Stop sharing disconnects everyone and the link stops working. Next time you get a new link.
  • If a friend closes the page or loses connection, they're fully disconnected and have to ask again.

4 Good to know

Private

The link has a long secret part, and nobody gets in without your OK. Video is encrypted and goes straight from your computer to each friend. It doesn't pass through Cloudflare, which only carries the page and the "can I join?" handshake.

Your internet upload

Each friend gets their own copy of the stream: about 5 Mbps per friend at 1080p, 3 Mbps at 720p. If friends see stuttering, lower Quality.

Voice chat

Discord and TeamSpeak sound is never shared, so talk there freely while you watch; friends won't hear themselves echoed back.

5 If a friend can't connect

Most home and mobile connections work directly. Some (strict office or school Wi-Fi, a few mobile carriers and internet providers) block direct connections, and the friend sees "Couldn't reach the host directly".

Add a relay server (TURN)

A relay passes the video along for those friends. Set one up once:

  1. Get relay details: either a Cloudflare TURN key (Cloudflare dashboard → Realtime → TURN; free allowance, then pay per GB) or your own coturn server.
  2. Popcorn menu → Network → Relay server settings… opens config.json. Fill in the "turn" section, for Cloudflare: 
    "turn": { "cloudflareKeyId": "…", "cloudflareApiToken": "…" }
    or for your own server: 
    "turn": { "urls": ["turn:relay.example.com:3478"], "username": "…", "credential": "…" }
  3. Save the file, then Stop sharing and Start sharing again.

With a relay you can also turn on Network → Hide my IP: video then always goes through the relay, so friends never see your internet address.

? Other problems

  • The link says it isn't active: you stopped sharing or restarted Movie night. Send the new link.
  • "Creating your link…" never finishes: check your internet connection; a firewall or antivirus may be blocking cloudflared, which Movie night uses to create links.
  • No sound for a friend: they should check the volume slider, and on iPhone turn off silent mode. If the page shows Start watching, they tap it once.
  • Windows asked about the firewall: allow Movie night, which lets video reach friends directly (the installer normally does this).
  • Ubuntu shows a black screen: log out and choose Ubuntu on Xorg with the ⚙ button on the login screen.

+ Tailscale mode (optional)

Already use Tailscale with your friends? Network → Tailscale only serves the stream on your tailnet address instead of a public link, so only devices on your Tailscale network can even ask to join. Everything else works the same.
